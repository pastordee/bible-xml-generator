I will speak with you.” 2[†]And as he spoke to me, e the Spirit entered into
me and f set me on my feet, and I heard him speaking to me. 3And he said

who have rebelled against me. h They and their fathers have transgressed
against me to this very day.
4The descendants also are i impudent and
stubborn: I send you to them, and you shall say to them, ‘Thus says the
Lord GOD.’ 5And j whether they hear or refuse to hear (for they are g a
rebellious house) k they will know that a prophet has been among them.
6And you, son of man, l be not afraid of them, nor be afraid of their words,

not afraid of their words, nor be dismayed at their looks, for they are a
rebellious house. 7And you shall speak my words to them, j whether they
hear or refuse to hear, for they are a rebellious house.
8[†]“But you, son of man, hear what I say to you. o Be not rebellious like
that rebellious house; open your mouth and p eat what I give you.” 9And
when I looked, behold, q a hand was stretched out to me, and behold, r a
scroll of a book was in it. 10And he spread it before me. And it had writing
s on the front and on the back, and there were written on it words of
lamentation and mourning and woe.
