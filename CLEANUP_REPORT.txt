Date: November 9, 2024
Issue: Extra text/commentary mixed in with cross-references

PROBLEM DESCRIPTION:
====================
During the extraction process, some files had Study Bible commentary text
and narrative descriptions mixed in with the pure cross-reference lines.

Example from 1chronicles_ch1_raw_formatted.txt (Line 18 before cleanup):
"15 h Josh. 14:6, 14; 15:13, 17; 1 Chr. captivity (Ezra 1:8, 11) and builder 
of the foundation of the second temple (Ezra 5:15–17). elsewhere (Ezra 3:2, 8; 
5:2; Neh. 12:1; Hag. 1:12, 14; 2:2, 23) as ment that Zerubbabel was the son..."

This line contained 643 characters of extra text about captivity, builders,
temples, and Zerubbabel - none of which are cross-references.

CLEANUP CRITERIA:
=================
Lines were removed if they met ANY of these conditions:

1. Did not start with the pattern: "verse# letter book:chapter"
   Example: "^\d+\s+[a-z]\s+" regex pattern

2. Did not contain any valid Bible book abbreviations

3. Were longer than 200 characters (normal cross-refs are much shorter)

4. Contained more than 3 narrative/commentary words such as:
   - Connecting words: the, and, of, in, to, for, with, as, was, were, etc.
   - Narrative words: captivity, builder, temple, prophet, king, covenant
