A Song of Praise for the Lord ’s Faithfulness to His People a A Psalm of Thanksgiving.
100 Make b a joyful shout to the Lord , all you lands!
2 Serve the Lord with gladness; Come before His presence with singing.
3 Know that the Lord , He is God; c It is He who has made us, and not we ourselves; d We are His people and the sheep of His pasture.
4 e Enter into His gates with thanksgiving, And into His courts with praise. Be thankful to Him, and bless His name.
5 For the Lord is good; f His mercy is everlasting, And His truth endures to all generations.
