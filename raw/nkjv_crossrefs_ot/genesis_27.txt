33 Then Isaac trembled exceedingly, and said, “Who? Where is the one who hunted game and brought it to me? I ate all of it before you came, and I have blessed him— ad and indeed he shall be blessed.”

37 Then Isaac answered and said to Esau, ag “Indeed I have made him your master, and all his brethren I have given to him as servants; with ah grain and wine I have sustained him. What shall I do now for you, my son?”

39 Then Isaac his father answered and said to him: “Behold, aj your dwelling shall be of the fatness of the earth, And of the dew of heaven from above.
