The Power of God in His Deliverance of Israel a
114 When b Israel went out of Egypt, The house of Jacob c from a people of strange language,
2 d Judah became His sanctuary, And Israel His dominion.
3 e The sea saw it and fled; f Jordan turned back.
4 g The mountains skipped like rams, The little hills like lambs.
5 h What ails you, O sea, that you fled? O Jordan, that you turned back?
6 O mountains, that you skipped like rams? O little hills, like lambs?
7 Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord, At the presence of the God of Jacob,
8 i Who turned the rock into a pool of water, The flint into a fountain of waters.
