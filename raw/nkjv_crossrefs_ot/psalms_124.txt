The Lord the Defense of His People A Song of Ascents. Of David.
124 “If it had not been the Lord who was on our a side,” b Let Israel now say—
2 “If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, When men rose up against us,
3 Then they would have c swallowed us alive, When their wrath was kindled against us;
4 Then the waters would have overwhelmed us, The stream would have gone over our soul;
5 Then the swollen waters Would have gone over our soul.”
6 Blessed be the Lord , Who has not given us as prey to their teeth.
7 d Our soul has escaped e as a bird from the snare of the fowlers; The snare is broken, and we have escaped.
8 f Our help is in the name of the Lord , g Who made heaven and earth.
