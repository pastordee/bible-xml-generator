Trust in the Salvation of the Lord To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David.
13 How long, O Lord ? Will You forget me forever? a How long will You hide Your face from me?
2 How long shall I take counsel in my soul, Having sorrow in my heart daily? How long will my enemy be exalted over me?
3 Consider and hear me, O Lord my God; b Enlighten my eyes, c Lest I sleep the sleep of death;
4 Lest my enemy say, “I have prevailed against him”; Lest those who trouble me rejoice when I am moved.
5 But I have trusted in Your mercy; My heart shall rejoice in Your salvation.
6 I will sing to the Lord , Because He has dealt bountifully with me.
