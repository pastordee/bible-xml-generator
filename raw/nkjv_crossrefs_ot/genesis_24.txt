18 z So she said, “Drink, my lord.” Then she quickly let her pitcher down to her hand, and gave him a drink.

21 And the man, wondering at her, remained silent so as to know whether aa the Lord had made his journey prosperous or not.

56 And he said to them, “Do not hinder me, since the Lord has prospered my way; send me away so that I may go to my master.”
57 So they said, “We will call the young woman and ask her personally.”
58 Then they called Rebekah and said to her, “Will you go with this man?” And she said, “I will go.”
