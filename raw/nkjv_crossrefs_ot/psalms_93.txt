The Eternal Reign of the Lord
93 The a Lord reigns, He is clothed with majesty; The Lord is clothed, b He has girded Himself with strength. Surely the world is established, so that it cannot be moved.
2 c Your throne is established from of old; You are from everlasting.
3 The floods have lifted up, O Lord , The floods have lifted up their voice; The floods lift up their waves.
4 d The Lord on high is mightier Than the noise of many waters, Than the mighty waves of the sea.
5 Your testimonies are very sure; Holiness adorns Your house, O Lord , forever.
