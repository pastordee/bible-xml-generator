BOOK ONE Psalms 1–41 The Way of the Righteous and the End of the Ungodly
1 Blessed a is the man Who walks not in the counsel of the ungodly, Nor stands in the path of sinners, b Nor sits in the seat of the scornful;
2 But c his delight is in the law of the Lord , d And in His law he meditates day and night.
3 He shall be like a tree e Planted by the rivers of water, That brings forth its fruit in its season, Whose leaf also shall not wither; And whatever he does shall f prosper.
4 The ungodly are not so, But are g like the chaff which the wind drives away.
5 Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, Nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.
6 For h the Lord knows the way of the righteous, But the way of the ungodly shall perish.
