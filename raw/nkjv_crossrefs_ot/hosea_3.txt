Israel Will Return to God
3 Then the Lord said to me, “Go again, love a woman who is loved by a a lover and is committing adultery, just like the love of the Lord for the children of Israel, who look to other gods and love the raisin cakes of the pagans. ”
2 So I bought her for myself for fifteen shekels of silver, and one and one-half homers of barley.
3 And I said to her, “You shall b stay with me many days; you shall not play the harlot, nor shall you have a man—so, too, will I be toward you.”
4 For the children of Israel shall abide many days c without king or prince, without sacrifice or sacred pillar, without d ephod or e teraphim.
5 Afterward the children of Israel shall return and f seek the Lord their God and g David their king. They shall fear the Lord and His goodness in the h latter days.
