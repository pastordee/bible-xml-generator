Praising the Lord in His House at Night A Song of Ascents.
134 Behold, bless the Lord , All you servants of the Lord , Who by night stand in the house of the Lord !
2 a Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, And bless the Lord .
3 The Lord who made heaven and earth Bless you from Zion!
