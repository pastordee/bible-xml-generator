John 3:1–5

You Must Be Born Again

  [1] Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews. [2] This man came to Jesus(1) by night and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher come from God, for no one can do these signs that you do unless God is with him.” [3] Jesus answered him, “Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born again(2) he cannot see the kingdom of God.” [4] Nicodemus said to him, “How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother’s womb and be born?” [5] Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.

Footnotes

(1) 3:2 Greek *him*

(2) 3:3 Or *from above*; the Greek is purposely ambiguous and can mean both *again* and *from above*; also verse 7
 (ESV)
