COLOSSIANS 4 [†]Masters, treat your bondservants justly and fairly,
knowing that you also have a Master in heaven.
Further Instructions
2 c Continue steadfastly in prayer, being watchful in it d with thanksgiving.
3[†]At the same time, pray also for us, that God may e open to us a door for

5[†] h Walk in wisdom toward i outsiders, making the best use of the time.
6Let your speech always j be gracious, k seasoned with salt, l so that you
