Walking in the Light
5[†] l This is the message we have heard from him and proclaim to you, that
m God is light, and in him is no darkness at all. 6[†] n If we say we have
fellowship with him while we walk in darkness, we lie and o do not practice
the truth. 7[†]But p if we walk in the light, q as he is in the light, we have
fellowship with one another, and r the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us
from all sin. 8[†] s If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and t the
truth is not in us. 9[†] u If we confess our sins, he is v faithful and just to
forgive us our sins and r to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. 10[†]If we
say we have not sinned, w we make him a liar, and x his word is not in us.
