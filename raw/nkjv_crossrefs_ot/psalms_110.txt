Announcement of the Messiah’s Reign a A Psalm of David.
110 The b Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at My right hand, Till I make Your enemies Your c footstool.”
2 The Lord shall send the rod of Your strength d out of Zion. e Rule in the midst of Your enemies!
3 f Your people shall be volunteers In the day of Your power; g In the beauties of holiness, from the womb of the morning, You have the dew of Your youth.
4 The Lord has sworn And h will not relent, “You are a i priest forever According to the order of j Melchizedek.”
5 The Lord is k at Your right hand; He shall execute kings l in the day of His wrath.
6 He shall judge among the nations, He shall fill the places with dead bodies, m He shall execute the heads of many countries.
7 He shall drink of the brook by the wayside; n Therefore He shall lift up the head.
