Blessings of Those Who Fear the Lord A Song of Ascents.
128 Blessed a is every one who fears the Lord , Who walks in His ways.
2 b When you eat the labor of your hands, You shall be happy, and it shall be c well with you.
3 Your wife shall be d like a fruitful vine In the very heart of your house, Your e children f like olive plants All around your table.
4 Behold, thus shall the man be blessed Who fears the Lord .
5 g The Lord bless you out of Zion, And may you see the good of Jerusalem All the days of your life.
6 Yes, may you h see your children’s children. i Peace be upon Israel!
