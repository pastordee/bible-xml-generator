Simple Trust in the Lord A Song of Ascents. Of David.
131 Lord , my heart is not haughty, Nor my eyes lofty. a Neither do I concern myself with great matters, Nor with things too profound for me.
2 Surely I have calmed and quieted my soul, b Like a weaned child with his mother; Like a weaned child is my soul within me.
3 c O Israel, hope in the Lord From this time forth and forever.
