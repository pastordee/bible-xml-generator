Example of Hagar and Sarah
21[†]Tell me, you who desire to be under the law, do you not listen to the
law? 22For it is written that Abraham had two sons, e one by a slave woman
and f one by a free woman. 23[†]But g the son of the slave was born
according to the flesh, while h the son of the free woman was born through
promise. 24Now this may be interpreted allegorically: these women are two

Hagar.
25[†]Now Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia; [5] she corresponds to the
present Jerusalem, for she is in slavery with her children. 26[†]But k the
Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.
