Laws About Altars
22[†]And the LORD said to Moses, “Thus you shall say to the people of
Israel: ‘You have seen for yourselves that I have b talked with you from
heaven. 23 c You shall not make gods of silver to be with me, nor shall you
make for yourselves gods of gold. 24An altar of earth you shall make for me
and sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep
and your oxen. d In every place where I cause my name to be remembered I
will come to you and e bless you. 25 f If you make me an altar of stone, g you
shall not build it of hewn stones, for if you wield your tool on it you profane
it. 26And you shall not go up by steps to my altar, that your nakedness be
not exposed on it.’
