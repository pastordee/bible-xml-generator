m two baskets of figs placed before the temple of the LORD.
2One basket had

4Then the word of the LORD came to me: 5“Thus says the LORD, the God
of Israel: Like these good figs, so I will regard as good the exiles from
Judah, p whom I have sent away from this place to the land of the
Chaldeans. 6[†] q I will set my eyes on them for good, and I will bring them

that I am the LORD, u and they shall be my people u and I will be their God,
v for they shall return to me with their whole heart.
8[†]“But thus says the LORD: Like w the bad figs that are so bad they

the land of Egypt. 9I will make them z a horror [1] to all the kingdoms of the
