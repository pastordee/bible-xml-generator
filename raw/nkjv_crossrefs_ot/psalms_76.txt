The Majesty of God in Judgment To the Chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.
76 In a Judah God is known; His name is great in Israel.
2 In Salem also is His tabernacle, And His dwelling place in Zion.
3 There He broke the arrows of the bow, The shield and sword of battle. Selah
4 You are more glorious and excellent b Than the mountains of prey.
5 c The stouthearted were plundered; d They have sunk into their sleep; And none of the mighty men have found the use of their hands.
6 e At Your rebuke, O God of Jacob, Both the chariot and horse were cast into a dead sleep.
7 You, Yourself, are to be feared; And f who may stand in Your presence When once You are angry?
8 g You caused judgment to be heard from heaven; h The earth feared and was still,
9 When God i arose to judgment, To deliver all the oppressed of the earth. Selah
10 j Surely the wrath of man shall praise You; With the remainder of wrath You shall gird Yourself.
11 k Make vows to the Lord your God, and pay them; l Let all who are around Him bring presents to Him who ought to be feared.
12 He shall cut off the spirit of princes; m He is awesome to the kings of the earth.
