C/V/c/i/m FORMAT STRUCTURE
--------------------------
C    Chapter {N}              - Chapter marker
V    {book}{chapter}{verse}   - Verse ID (e.g., 01001001 = Genesis 1:1)
c    {letter}                 - Cross-reference letter marker
i    c{verse_id}.{counter}    - Unique cross-reference ID
m    {reference text}         - Cross-reference content

Example (Genesis 1:1):
C       Chapter 1
V       01001001
c       a
i       c01001001.1
m       Ps. 102:25; Is. 40:21; [John 1:1-3; Heb. 1:10]
