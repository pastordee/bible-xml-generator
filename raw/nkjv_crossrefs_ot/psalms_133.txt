Blessed Unity of the People of God A Song of Ascents. Of David.
133 Behold, how good and how pleasant it is For a brethren to dwell together in unity!
2 It is like the precious oil upon the head, Running down on the beard, The beard of Aaron, Running down on the edge of his garments.
3 It is like the dew of b Hermon, Descending upon the mountains of Zion; For c there the Lord commanded the blessing— Life forevermore.
