Bildad: How Can Man Be Righteous?
25 Then a Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:
2 “Dominion and fear belong to Him; He makes peace in His high places.
3 Is there any number to His armies? Upon whom does b His light not rise?
4 c How then can man be righteous before God? Or how can he be d pure who is born of a woman?
5 If even the moon does not shine, And the stars are not pure in His e sight,
6 How much less man, who is f a maggot, And a son of man, who is a worm?”
