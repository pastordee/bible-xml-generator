God the Help of Those Who Seek Him A Song of Ascents.
121 I a will lift up my eyes to the hills— From whence comes my help?
2 b My help comes from the Lord , Who made heaven and earth.
3 c He will not allow your foot to be moved; d He who keeps you will not slumber.
4 Behold, He who keeps Israel Shall neither slumber nor sleep.
5 The Lord is your keeper; The Lord is e your shade f at your right hand.
6 g The sun shall not strike you by day, Nor the moon by night.
7 The Lord shall preserve you from all evil; He shall h preserve your soul.
8 The Lord shall i preserve your going out and your coming in From this time forth, and even forevermore.
