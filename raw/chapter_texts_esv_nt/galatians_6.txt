5For x each will have to bear his own load.

g as we have opportunity, let us h do good to everyone, and especially to

Final Warning and Benediction
11[†]See with what large letters I am writing to you j with my own hand.
12
k It is those who want to make a good showing in the flesh l who would

for anything, nor uncircumcision, but q a new creation. 16[†]And as for all
who walk by this rule, r peace and mercy be upon them, and upon s the

marks of Jesus.
18[†] t The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be u with your spirit, brothers.
